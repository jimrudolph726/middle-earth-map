I

Fan-project status

Tolkien Legendarium Atlas is an unofficial fan-made project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or authorized by the Tolkien Estate, Middle-earth Enterprises, Warner Bros., New Line Cinema, HarperCollins, or their affiliates. Tolkien-related names, characters, places, writings, artwork, and trademarks remain the property of their respective rights holders.

This is a personal, non-commercial project created for exploration and study.

No use of a name, character, place, quotation, image, or mark should be understood as a claim of ownership, endorsement, or permission.

II

Lore and map sources

The atlas is an interpretive companion to Tolkien's legendarium. Tolkien-related names, places, events, genealogies, and narratives originate in the published works of J.R.R. Tolkien and the editorial work associated with those publications.

Many entries provide a further-reading link to Tolkien Gateway. Those links are references for readers, not a claim that Tolkien Gateway sponsors or endorses this project.

An entry-by-entry source review is ongoing so that original summaries, adapted material, and any quotations can be distinguished more precisely. The maps are designed for exploration and should not be treated as authoritative editions or exact geographical reconstructions.

III

Artwork and image provenance

The site contains map images, location illustrations, portraits, crests, icons, and decorative textures. A per-file image register is being assembled to record each asset's creator, source, creation method, and reuse terms.

Some legacy assets do not yet have sufficiently documented provenance. They are under review and may be credited more fully, replaced, or removed. The presence of an image in this repository does not grant permission to extract or reuse it.

Middle-earth physical-volume materials

IV

AI-generated artwork

Some visual assets—including the homepage study illustration and Story Mode artwork—were created with generative image tools under the direction of the project creator. They are original fan-project presentation assets, not official Tolkien or film artwork.

The completed journeys have per-file illustration registers for Sam and Frodo and Gandalf the White, recording the generation tool, creation date, shared visual direction, exact scene prompts, corrections, and post-processing. AI-assisted work is identified instead of being presented as historical or rights-holder artwork.

Atlas sounds

The wood click, parchment flick, quill stroke, and book thump recordings come from Mixkit's free sound-effects library and are used under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License. Their item IDs, source categories, and download date appear in the audio credits ledger. The recordings may not be redistributed as a standalone sound-effects collection.

VII

Repository license

No repository-wide reuse license has yet been declared. Until one is added, public availability of the source does not itself grant permission to copy, modify, or redistribute it.

Any future repository license will apply only to original project code and material that the project creator has the right to license. Tolkien-related text, names, maps, imagery, likenesses, fonts, audio, and other third-party assets will remain excluded unless an individual asset notice explicitly says otherwise.

VIII

Corrections and rights-holder contact

If you created or own rights in material presented here, or if you identify an incomplete or incorrect credit, please open a GitHub issue. Include the relevant page or asset path, the nature of the concern, and a reliable way to verify the claim. Good-faith correction and removal requests will be reviewed promptly.

For information from the rights holders themselves, consult the Tolkien Estate's guidance, Middle-earth Enterprises licensing information, or Warner Bros. clip and still licensing.
