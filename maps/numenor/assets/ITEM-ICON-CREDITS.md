# Númenor item medallions

`diamond-of-erendis-medallion.svg` and `gil-galads-letter-medallion.svg`
are original code-drawn SVG illustrations created for this atlas in September
2026. Their indigo fields and bronze rims follow the existing Númenor city
medallion. No external raster artwork or font is embedded.

The diamond's cut and the letter's appearance are artistic interpretations,
not documented reconstructions. In particular, the blue wax seal and ribbon
do not assert a canonical seal color or a heraldic device of Gil-galad.
