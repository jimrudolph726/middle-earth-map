# Frontispiece ornaments

The Middle-earth, Beleriand, and Númenor `*-frontispiece-ornament.svg` files
are original code-drawn illustrations created for this atlas in September
2026. They interpret the established leaf-and-road, star-and-wave, and
compass-star themes; they are not claims of canonical heraldic designs.
No third-party artwork or fonts are embedded.

The Shire's existing barley-and-acorn illustration is documented in
`../the_shire/assets/CREDITS.md`. All four frontispieces share the same
120 × 64px illustration layout in `volume-introduction.css`.
